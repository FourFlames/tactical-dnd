# Playtest notes

Running list from play sessions, newest first. Turn the ones worth doing into cards in HANDOFF.md.

## 2026-10-13: goblin-warren-caves in the sandbox, user plays Rook, Wren and Ash on one seat

17. **Reaction visible off-turn; OAs are the player's choice.** After Rook's opportunity attack his HUD still showed nothing (pips only draw on your own turn). The reaction pip should always show in combat, and a provoked OA should be offered to the player (Do it / Never mind), not rolled by the DM. (Sonnet, in progress.)
16. *(DM slip)* **No way back a turn.** I ran `next` one time too many and skipped Wren's round-3 turn; the engine has no `prev`/`turn <id>`, so the DM can't repair the order. Also: when a mind's move PROVOKES, the engine just warns; the OA has to be rolled by hand after the fact (needed a `place` back and forth). Card 8's "monster OAs rolled on the move" covers the second half.
15. **Right-click is the ring's alternate menu.** Right-clicking a slot opens that slot's alternatives (the other attacks/spells/features that could live there). In that menu, left-click uses the option right away; right-click swaps it into the slot (remembered for that character). Queued after the action-type fix.
14. **Tracks.** Survival and tracking could become really cool with creatures leaving tracks (footprints in mud/guano/blood, how old, how many, which way), read with Survival.
13. **Sound events need a source.** "Rook and Brother Ash hear a voice to the east shout..." should carry where it came from (a cell chip / direction marker on the map), as far as the hearer can tell.
12. **Stealth needs a lot of cover.** Maps for sneaking want far more cover/concealment: pillars, crates, stalagmites, dim pockets. Card 9's Warren rework should budget for it.
11. **Spiritual Weapon should get a body** on the map (a spectral token that moves 20 ft with its bonus action), later.
10. *(spotted by Claude)* **Morale breaks don't move the minds.** After Snikka fell, 7 goblins failed the DM-notes Wis DC 10 and got `mind <id> notice "...your nerve breaks..."`, but on their turns they kept their old plans (the Lookout kept shooting, Mogwa kept guarding). A rout needs a real lever: a `mind <id> rout` / `flee` order or a morale state the planner honours.
9. **Spells on the wheel ignore the action economy.** With Ash's action already spent on a mace swing, the wheel still offered Sacred Flame and sent it as a request ("casts Sacred Flame at Gix"). Spell slots should know their cost (action/bonus/reaction) and grey out like Dodge does when that part of the turn is spent.
8. *(spotted by Claude)* **Spiritual Weapon spent Ash's action.** It's in his `attacks` list, so the wheel's Strike runs a plain `attack`, which spends the action; it should be a bonus action (and the first use casts it: a 2nd-level slot). Patched by hand with `regain ash action` + `use ash bonus`. Also no engine command spends a resource (his `slots2` still reads 1 after casting); the DM needs one. Attacks need an `as: bonus` (or similar) field the server honours.
7. **Undo should restore hiding**, and before undoing, say what the undone move revealed (new squares, creatures, traps) and ask "sure?": undo is for misclicks, not for unseeing things. If an NPC noticed the mover, they stay seen. Done: undo restores hiding unless an NPC spotted the mover; a second click lists what the undone move showed.
6. **Wheel ring follows the zoom.** Zoomed out, the ring stayed 70 px+ around a tiny square, so the fold cutoff was far away and the wheel clung. Fixed in pip-wheel.js: buttons keep their size, the ring hugs the target (44-140 px), and below 70 px only the hot button's caption shows.
5. **Full-window map, everything overlays.** A status line ("Brother Ash moves…") appearing above the map pushed the viewport down and the 3D view redrew. Done: map fills the window; top bar, status strip, sidebar (collapsible ‹ ›), HUD and look card float over it. Unchecked: narrow windows, initiative strip vs top bar around 1000 px.
4. **Clicking a traced icon must not miss.** After tracing a stem to its icon, a click that visually lands on the icon sometimes didn't pick it. Fixed in marks3d.js: a lone traced/hovered icon now grows like icon-mode ones (up to 1.5×), hit radius 1.25-1.5× drawn and at least 20 px for the icon you're on.
3. *(spotted by Claude, fixed)* After `load`, the Story tab still shows the previous game's player messages ("tester (Rook): test hi", Action Surge...) between the new intro lines. Old intents seem to leak into a fresh game's story; they should belong to the game they were sent in. The "DM is resolving…" strip also sat there with nothing pending.
2. **Placeholders for the void / unseen.** In 3D at the landing you get a few lit squares floating in pure black, with the column letters A-Z strung across the dark on their own. It's jarring. Wanted: something in place of the unknown, e.g. a dim fog or rough rock mass over unseen cells, a faint edge where known turns into unknown, and the axis labels hidden or tied to what's known. (Overlaps Card 9's "rock masses / backdrop past the map edge".)
1. **Move with the wheel out of combat.** Everything on the wheel keys off `myTurn()` (viewer.html ~1373), which needs a turn order, and the server's turn helper (server.js ~23) returns null without one. So before initiative a seated player can't move at all except by asking the DM. Wanted: before initiative, the selected seated creature (one seat can hold several) can move/sneak/open doors from the wheel. No turn budget, but an exploration step stays one round's worth of movement, and the DM `tick`s.
